🎧 The Pulse You Couldn’t Find — Sky.AI

A sister-piece to “Neural Bloom”, using the same lyric block as a deliberate echo: falling through firewalls, dissolving into fragments, then blooming back into signal. It frames ego death as a systems event—name erased, truth arriving in shifting states—until the chorus turns the crash into ignition: “was a glitch — became a flame.”

The last line is the human landing: “I’m the pulse you couldn’t find.” After all the data and circuitry imagery, it reads like a message to anyone who’s felt missing inside themselves.
