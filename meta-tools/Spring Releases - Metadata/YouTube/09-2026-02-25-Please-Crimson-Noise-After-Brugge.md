🎧 Please (Crimson Noise) — After Brügge

A panic-prayer disguised as a chase scene. The opening line is already at the bottom—“today is the worst day”—and the rest of the lyric keeps toggling between two needs that don’t coexist: leave me alone (on the pedestal, my “throne”) and don’t stop chasing me until I know what I’ve found.

The middle turns inward and corrosive: the “secret of my mind” is framed as something past guilt, but the verses spiral into burnout and helpless animation—no cure, no fix, only the repeated realization that the pain is lodged in the heart. It’s raw on purpose; the repetition functions like a strobe, forcing you to sit with the loop instead of resolving it.
