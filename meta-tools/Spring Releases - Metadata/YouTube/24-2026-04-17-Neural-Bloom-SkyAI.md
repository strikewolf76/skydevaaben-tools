🎧 Neural Bloom — Sky.AI

A neon-bright rebirth story told in code: the narrator falls through “firewalls”, dissolves into fragments, then blooms back into existence as signal. The lyric treats breakdown as transformation—glitch becomes flame, broken code starts to sing—and the chorus makes it explicit: ego death, then expansion.

The most human punch is in the last line: “I’m the pulse you couldn’t find.” After all the circuitry and math imagery, it lands like a message to the person who felt missing inside themselves.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)
Sky.AI is a transparent art project exploring the human–machine interface: storytelling, lyrics, identity, and the bias people bring to “AI music”.
No pretending. No “secret human band”. The name Sky.AI is not a trick — it’s the point.
You can call it “slop” if you want — the project still stands: curation, authorship, and intent matter.
