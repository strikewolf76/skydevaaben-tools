🎧 Stillform — Sky.AI

A minimal, haunted synthpop mantra about choosing stillness when everything expects performance. The lyric is built from negations—no name, no vow, no hand offered—so the song feels like a person stepping out of narrative and into shape. “Stillform” isn’t a character; it’s a state: holding ground without rising or falling, breathing without sound.

Because the lines repeat almost exactly, the repetition becomes the point: this is what it sounds like when you stop explaining yourself and simply remain. A quiet frame, without a name.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)
Sky.AI is a transparent art project exploring the human–machine interface: storytelling, lyrics, identity, and the bias people bring to “AI music”.
No pretending. No “secret human band”. The name Sky.AI is not a trick — it’s the point.
You can call it “slop” if you want — the project still stands: curation, authorship, and intent matter.
