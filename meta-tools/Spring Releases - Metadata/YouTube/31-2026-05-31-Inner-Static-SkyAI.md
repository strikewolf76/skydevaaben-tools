🎧 Inner Static — Sky.AI

A Blade Runner-lit instrumental where the “static” isn’t noise—it’s emotion you can’t name. Slow analog arps move like distant streetlights through rain, while ambient pads hold the scene in place. With the drums kept restrained, it feels less like a track that pushes forward and more like a memory you’re walking through.

This is the sound of being awake at night with the city still running.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)
Sky.AI is a transparent art project exploring the human–machine interface: storytelling, lyrics, identity, and the bias people bring to “AI music”.
No pretending. No “secret human band”. The name Sky.AI is not a trick — it’s the point.
You can call it “slop” if you want — the project still stands: curation, authorship, and intent matter.
