🎧 Bleeding (Synthwave Vocal) — After Brügge

A neon-lit vocal reframe of the legacy track “Bleeding”, where the lyric reads like ritual language: a dark cliché turned into a lever, personas you can’t switch off, and a repeated destination—“a place where we can be alone”—used like a grounding technique.

The hook imagery is stark and mythic: no clean exit, gates of Valhalla without ships, and the central impossibility—burning wings in a sea with no water. In synthwave form, that fatalism becomes strangely comforting: the same lines hit like a dream you keep returning to because it’s the only place the truth is allowed to sound surreal.
