🎧 My Mind Photography (Crimson Noise) — After Brügge

A rawer, shouted take on the same trapped-thought lyric: the narrator can’t stop replaying the same internal image (“mind photography”), and every loop ends in the same sentence: “I can’t get out.” It’s not storytelling so much as being stuck inside a camera flash—memory turned into a cage.

The most brutal line is almost casual: “Too late to rewind.” Not regret as emotion, but as a hard limit.
