🎧 Low (Annihilate) — After Brügge

A dark-ambient reduction of the “Low” narrative: less story, more pressure. What remains of the text is the rawest core—failure, hollow empires, and the chorus as a single command: drown the old hymns, burn off the wasted lies.

Because only a Verse + Chorus excerpt is preserved for this version, it reads like a fragment pulled from the loudest moment and looped as a mantra. The title-word “Annihilate” becomes less a threat and more a boundary: the point where the narrator stops explaining and starts cutting.
