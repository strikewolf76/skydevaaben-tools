🎧 Bleeding (Crimson Noise) — After Brügge

A colder, more atmospheric rework of the legacy track “Bleeding”, where the lyric feels like mythic exhaustion filtered through surreal syntax. The opening images frame identity as a system you can’t disable—persona displays, fragile days turned into levers—then the song pivots into refuge: “I know a place where we can be alone”, repeated like a safety spell.

From there it escalates into doomed-hero imagery: no clean exit, no valid excuse, approaching Valhalla without ships or valkyries. The most striking paradox lands in the center—burning wings in a sea with no water—turning survival into impossible physics. The final refrain returns to that hidden place and the dark joke at the end: jam another dragon down the hole.
