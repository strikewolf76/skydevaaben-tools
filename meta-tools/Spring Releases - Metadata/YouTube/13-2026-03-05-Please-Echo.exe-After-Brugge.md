🎧 Please (Echo.exe) — After Brügge

An instrumental-only “Please” variant: same emotional silhouette, but the words are removed, leaving just the echo of the hook. With no vocals, it reads like the aftermath of the lyric versions—what’s left when the argument stops and your nervous system keeps replaying the scene.

The logged brief is crisp synthwave with arpeggios, so the track leans into motion and clarity: neon lines, steady propulsion, and a melody shaped like something you almost remember saying. It’s the plea translated into pure signal.
