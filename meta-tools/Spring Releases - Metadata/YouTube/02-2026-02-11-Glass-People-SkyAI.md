🎧 Glass People — Sky.AI

A melancholic synthpop snapshot of digital-era invisibility: moving through crowds “like glass”, watched but not met, present but not remembered. The lyrics treat identity as a signal problem—your name becomes “static in their stream”, your presence something that pixelates, glitches, and slips off the edge of everyone else’s attention.

The chorus turns the metaphor into a manifesto: “clear but unseeable” isn’t weakness, it’s a kind of persistence. Even when nobody looks, the narrator still burns blue, still glows—cracked, but intact. The final verse refuses the disappearing act: don’t look away; there’s light behind the crystal skin, and everything you missed within.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)
Sky.AI is a transparent art project exploring the human–machine interface: storytelling, lyrics, identity, and the bias people bring to “AI music”.
No pretending. No “secret human band”. The name Sky.AI is not a trick — it’s the point.
You can call it “slop” if you want — the project still stands: curation, authorship, and intent matter.
