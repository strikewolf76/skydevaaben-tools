🎧 Low (Crimson Noise) — After Brügge

A riff-driven descent where the lyric vocabulary is almost entirely impact language: failing, empty, hollow, broke—then the chorus flips into drowning/suffocating imagery like a ritual of erasure. The narrator isn’t negotiating; it’s an exorcism of whatever keeps feeding “wasted lies”.

This version’s hook leans harder into annihilation as the terminal word. The emotional logic is brutal and simple: when the old “psalms” are poison, you don’t reform them—you take them all the way down.
