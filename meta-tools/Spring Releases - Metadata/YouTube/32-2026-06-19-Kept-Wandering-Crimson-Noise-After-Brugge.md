🎧 Kept Wandering (Crimson Noise) — After Brügge

A slow-burning Scandinavian alt-rock sermon about losing the map and calling it a home. The lyric opens with a brutal contrast—soft “mad children” laughing at sanity—then admits the real descent: raving deeper into a private jungle of madness and loathing.

The hook is simple because the situation is simple: you don’t “find yourself” in this world, you keep moving. And the second verse twists the knife with a generational image—“eating the cancers of our ancestors”—like inherited damage becoming daily food. The chorus answers every hope with the same verdict: “Not a fucking chance.”
