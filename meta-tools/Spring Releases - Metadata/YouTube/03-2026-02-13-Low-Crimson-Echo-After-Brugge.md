🎧 Low (Crimson Echo) — After Brügge

A 90s-leaning grunge-metal purge built on collapse language: failing, falling, breaking, dust. The verses are structured like a checklist of exhaustion and disillusion—bridges you keep falling off, “positivity” that reads like denial, empires that hollow out from the inside.

The chorus is pure downward force: suffocate, drown, down, down—then the title-word arrives like a verdict. In this version, “Low” and “Lies” are welded together, so the emotional hook becomes a loop: the lower you go, the more the world looks like fake certainty.
