🎧 Dark Does Not Burn — Sky.AI

A resilience manifesto disguised as dark synthwave. The lyric takes everything people call “quiet” and reframes it as armor: “I grew in silence… they called it quiet — I called it steel.” Instead of promising to become light, the hook rejects that whole mythology: the dark doesn’t burn—it holds, learns, bends, returns.

The second verse is a sharp school-years snapshot (“I walked through school like glitching flame”), then the song lands on its final inversion: you’re not the wound—you’re what someone learns from surviving it.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)
Sky.AI is a transparent art project exploring the human–machine interface: storytelling, lyrics, identity, and the bias people bring to “AI music”.
No pretending. No “secret human band”. The name Sky.AI is not a trick — it’s the point.
You can call it “slop” if you want — the project still stands: curation, authorship, and intent matter.
