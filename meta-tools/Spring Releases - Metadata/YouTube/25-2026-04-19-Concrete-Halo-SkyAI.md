🎧 Concrete Halo — Sky.AI

A love letter to the quiet kind of loyalty—the person who doesn’t rescue you with speeches, but stays anyway. The lyric keeps refusing grand mythology (“you didn’t save me like a saint”), then reframes it as something deeper: being there *is* a choice, a steady glow that holds when everything else collapses.

“Concrete halo” is the perfect contradiction: something holy made from weight. Cracked, practical, and still protective.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)
Sky.AI is a transparent art project exploring the human–machine interface: storytelling, lyrics, identity, and the bias people bring to “AI music”.
No pretending. No “secret human band”. The name Sky.AI is not a trick — it’s the point.
You can call it “slop” if you want — the project still stands: curation, authorship, and intent matter.
