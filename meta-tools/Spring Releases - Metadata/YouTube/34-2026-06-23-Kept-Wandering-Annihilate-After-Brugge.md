🎧 Kept Wandering (Annihilate) — After Brügge

A coldwave/post-punk conversion of the Kept Wandering lyric: same words, but the emotion is flattened into something mechanical—dry drum machines, hypnotic bass, and a slow build that feels like tension you can’t spend.

In that frame, the lyric hits like a diagnosis: we kept wandering because stopping would mean looking straight at the madness and the inheritance (“eating the cancers of our ancestors”). The chorus becomes repetition-as-evidence: you don’t say it four times because it’s catchy, you say it because it’s true.
