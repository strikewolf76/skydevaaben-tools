🎧 King Egosentric (Crimson Echo) — After Brügge

A tight, dry, funk-locked alt-rock remaster where obsession and ego read like a confession you can’t take back. The lyric keeps twisting intimacy into fuel—"all your loving words / as gasolin"—and turns the gaze into a weapon: distant eyes pinned on you, repeating until it feels like surveillance.

The chorus flips desire into cruelty (“just to hear you cry again”), but it also reads like dependence: shadows only appear when the other person breaks. That’s the core tension here—power-posturing vs. a starving need for reaction—then the bridge lands like a motto: “life given / life taken.”
