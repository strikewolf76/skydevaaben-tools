🎧 My Mind Photography (Crimson Echo) — After Brügge

A panic-loop punk track built from two sentences that won’t let go: “My mind photography” and “I can’t get out.” The lyric feels like being trapped inside your own snapshots—too many pieces lost, too many pieces caged—where the past isn’t memory, it’s a room you keep waking up in.

Because the lines repeat, the repetition becomes the psychology: not a chorus for singing along, but a thought pattern. The Crimson Echo mix brief keeps it gritty but balanced, with the vocal slightly lower—like the voice is inside the noise instead of on top of it.
