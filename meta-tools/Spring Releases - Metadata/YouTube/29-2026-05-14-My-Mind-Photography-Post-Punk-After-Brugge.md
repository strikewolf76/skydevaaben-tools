🎧 My Mind Photography (Post Punk) — After Brügge

Same lyric, different knife: this version leans into thick guitar layers and an angry, desperate vocal, making the repetition feel less like a loop and more like an accusation. “Too many pieces of me caged” lands like self-contempt, and “I can’t get out” becomes something shouted at the walls.

The song is basically one obsession rendered in three phrases—lost, caged, trapped—and the post-punk framing makes it sound like the room is getting smaller every time the chorus returns.
