🎧 Fire Without A Flame — Sky.AI

A dark-synth resilience statement that refuses the usual “light wins” framing. The lyric thesis is blunt: darkness doesn’t burn—it holds, it learns, it bends. Instead of pretending pain didn’t happen, the narrator treats it like training data: silence becomes steel, numbness becomes discipline, and survival becomes a kind of perception (“I’m not the light / but I discern”).

The verses sketch a childhood/teenage survival mode—walking through school like a glitching flame, building invisible walls—then the refrain returns as a repeated redefinition. By the end, the song flips the wound into lesson: not the flame, not the wound—what returns; what you learn.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)
