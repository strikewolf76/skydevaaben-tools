🎧 All Clear Now — Sky.AI

A high-energy synthwave/trance catharsis built around a decade-long “static → signal” transformation. The narrator wakes “mid-collapse” and reframes the damage as engineering: pain becomes a compass, pressure becomes fate. The lyric arc climbs from hallway survival (names thrown like knives) into a summit-state where scars turn to lift (cuts → feathers, scars → wings), and clarity arrives like ignition.

The chorus lands as earned self-definition: you don’t just change—you come back with a name. The hook’s contrast is the whole story: cold at the summit, warm in your gaze; clarity isn’t comfort, it’s contact.

🤖 What is Sky.AI (and what it is NOT)

No pretending. No “secret human band”. The name Sky.AI is not a trick — it’s the point.
