🎧 Kept Wandering (Crimson Echo) — After Brügge

The same bleak thesis, sharpened: sanity is something you watch from a distance while you sink deeper into your own jungle. The lyric is almost documentary—no metaphor escape hatches—then the hook lands like a stamp you can’t remove: “We kept wandering.”

The second verse expands the horror from personal to civilizational: living among psychopaths without realizing we’re consuming inherited rot (“the cancers of our ancestors”). And when the chorus breaks into “Without knowing… Not a fucking chance”, it feels like the moment denial finally dies.

This Crimson Echo remaster brief is ruthless about integrity: same structure, bar count, melody, phrasing—just a cleaner, wider mix. No new sections, no new riffs. The song is the same wound, just better lit.
