🎧 I Forgive You (Liminality) — Sky.AI

A single-breath vow written like a hand on your shoulder in a burning room. The lyric is all urgency and steadiness—“do not crash, do not burn”—but it refuses the usual demand for transformation: “you do not need to change… you just need to remain.” Forgiveness here isn’t erasing damage; it’s choosing presence anyway.

Even the imperfect phrasing (“dont not fall”, “i for give you”) makes it feel more human: raw, spoken, unedited—like something you say when you mean it.
