## 7. Style & Musical Character (as logged)

**Style prompt / descriptors:**  
A melodic synthwave / dreamwave Commodore 64 retro game OST reinterpretation of the song "Please" by After Brügge. No guitars. Keep the original vocal melody and structure closely. Avoid vocal improvisation, humming, or abstract phrasing. Use an emotionally sincere male vocal tone, slightly deep and melancholic (M83 / Mariupol vibe). Light natural reverb—not robotic, not overprocessed. Nostalgic analog-synth palette: warm bass arpeggiators, bright polysynth leads, soft gated drums. Verses harmonic and non-invasive; chorus blooms wide with emotional lead synths and reverb-washed percussion. Audio influence: LukHash; upbeat Commodore 64 / retro synth music; Mariupol – Lovers; Chris Huelsbeck - Turrican 2: Final Fight. Exclusions: -EDM, -trance, -dubstep, -metal, -pop punk, -cinematic score, -ambient drone, -hyperpop.
